A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.16.1: a new panel**
- **Search every rug.** Type in the bar at the top of the panel (or press Ctrl+F, ⌘F on a Mac): a rug's name, a town, a country or a pack. "Greece" finds all sixteen Greek rugs, "Istanbul" the Bosphorus Ferry.
- **On your desktop.** The rug that's down now is at the top of the panel, with where it comes from, and its size, weight and the rest right under it.
- **Recent rugs.** The last few rugs you used, one click away.
- **Surprise me.** Lays down a rug at random and opens its pack.
- **A calmer gallery.** Every pack on the same dark floor, so the rugs carry the colour. The pack you're in has a fringe under it, the tabs stay on top while you scroll, and the rugs are bigger, with the town they come from.
- **The license folds away.** Once it's active, it becomes a small tag with your key and the days left; the rest is under "Details". When something needs your attention, it's back on top.
- Slim scrollbars.

If you have 1.16.0, this update is a few MB and restarting takes a few seconds.

**From 1.16.0:** much smaller updates, a smaller app, a new update card and update screen.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
