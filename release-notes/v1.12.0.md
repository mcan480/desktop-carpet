A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.12.0: updates as soon as you open it**
- **Checks when it opens.** Desktop Carpet now looks for a new version the moment it starts, also when it starts with your computer, and again whenever you open it from the desktop, the Start menu, the Dock or the tray. If the network isn't up yet, it tries again a little later.
- **You can see it coming.** While a new version downloads, the panel shows a bar that fills up. When it's done, the bar turns into "Restart and update".
- **A heads-up when the panel is closed.** If the panel isn't open, you get one notification when the new version is ready. Click it to restart into the new version.
- **No double downloads.** A version that was downloaded but not installed yet is used again on the next start, after checking it's still the exact released file.

**From 1.11.0: a roomier panel**
A wider panel with bigger rugs, a tab for each collection (Classics, Halloween, Americas, Persia) and a framed showroom for the Persian rugs.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
