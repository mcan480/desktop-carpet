A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.11.0: a roomier panel**
- **Bigger panel, bigger rugs.** The panel opens wider, with your license and settings on the left and the rugs on the right, about twice the size they were. On a narrow window it falls back to one column.
- **Collections:** Classics, Halloween, Americas and Persia each have their own tab with a cover rug. The panel opens on the collection of the rug you're using.
- **The Persia showroom:** the Persian rugs hang in a framed vitrine. The one you're using is shown large with the town it comes from, and pointing at another one previews it there.

**From 1.10.0: the Persia pack**
*From the carpet capital, knot by knot.* Twelve knotted rugs from the carpet heartland, in their own section of the panel: Heriz, Paradise Garden, Hunting Scene, Tree of Life, Boteh, Bakhtiari, Qashqai Gabbeh, Nain, Kerman Vase, Herati, Qom Silk and Turkmen Gul.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
