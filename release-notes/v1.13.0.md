A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.13.0: the Türkiye pack**
*Between two continents, one rug at a time.* Twelve new rugs, right at the top of the panel.
- **Anatolian carpets:** Ushak Medallion, Lotto, Bergama, Yağcıbedir, Seljuk, Hereke Silk and Iznik Tulips, knotted on a fine grid like the real thing.
- **Picture rugs:** Greetings from Türkiye, Cappadocia Balloons, Bosphorus Ferry, Pamukkale and Nazar Bead, hooked loop by loop.
- **Its own room:** the pack opens in a glazed İznik-tile room. The rug you're using hangs large with the town it comes from, and pointing at another one previews it there. The first time you open the panel after updating, it opens right on the new pack.
- Also in the tray menu, under Pattern.

**From 1.12.0:** Desktop Carpet looks for a new version as soon as it opens, and shows the download in the panel.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
