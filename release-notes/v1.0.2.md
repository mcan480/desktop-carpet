A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it.

**What's new in 1.0.2**
- Folds look right: the top and bottom of the rug no longer slip through each other when you fold it or drag it fast.

**Install:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it, then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com. If you already have 1.0.1, the app updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*.
