A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.17.1: a rug that lands and stays put**
- **No more turning on its own.** After you let go, a fold could keep rolling over or opening up by itself and turn the whole rug with it for several seconds. Now the rug comes to rest within a second or two, folds and all, and it's always still within 3 seconds.
- **No folds where you didn't touch it.** When you drag the rug around fast, it's now carried lower, close to the floor, so it slides along instead of swinging around and folding over at its far end. Slow down or stop and the corner you're holding lifts up again as before.
- Quick flicks still fold the rug over, and those folds now stay more often.

If you have 1.16.0 or later, this update is a few MB and restarting takes a few seconds.

**From 1.17.0:** the Nordic pack, 60 rugs from Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Iceland.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
