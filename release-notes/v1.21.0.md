A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.21.0: Persia and Türkiye grow to 24 rugs each**
- **Persia** gets six more carpets and its first picture rugs, now on two shelves.
  - *Persian carpets:* Sarouk, Bijar, Sultanabad, Joshaghan, Hamadan and Khamseh Birds.
  - *Picture rugs:* Si-o-se-pol Bridge, Persepolis, Yazd Windcatchers, Damavand Poppies, Persian Cat and a Greetings from Persia postcard, with a lapis and turquoise frame.
- **Türkiye** gets twelve more.
  - *Anatolian carpets:* Bird Ushak, Large-Pattern Holbein, Ottoman Court Carpet, Çanakkale and Yörük Nomad.
  - *Picture rugs:* Ebru Marbling, Safranbolu Houses, Library of Celsus, Rize Tea Gardens, Blue Voyage, Colourful Balat and Turkish Breakfast.
- Search finds the new rugs by name ("Holbein", "cat", "breakfast"), and they're in the tray menu under Pattern.

If you have 1.16.0 or later, this update is a few MB and restarting takes a few seconds.

**From 1.20.0:** Halloween grew to 24 rugs and Classics to 12.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
