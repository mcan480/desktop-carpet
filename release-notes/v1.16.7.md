A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.16.7: a rug that behaves like one**
- **It can't pass through itself.** Fold it over, twist it, drag it around: the layers stack on top of each other like a real rug's.
- **Lighter on your computer.** The rug's physics takes less than half the work it did, so moving it around no longer slows the computer down. If a computer still can't keep up, the rug moves a little slower instead of holding up the desktop.
- **It drapes instead of wadding up.** Held in the air, the rug hangs from your hand and never rises above it, so swinging it round doesn't crumple it into a ball.
- **Folds stay folded.** A rug you fold over stays that way; only a rug twisted into a real wad is laid back out flat when you let go. Double-click the rug to lay it flat yourself.

Your rug opens where you left it, laid flat.

If you have 1.16.0 or later, this update is a few MB and restarting takes a few seconds.

**From 1.16.1:** search every rug, the rug in use at the top of the panel, recent rugs, Surprise me and a calmer gallery.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
