A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.22.0: Jordan and Morocco grow to 24 rugs each**
- **Jordan** gets twelve more.
  - *Patterns and crafts:* Camel Caravan, Dead Sea Salt, Red Sea Fish, Desert Wildflowers, Petra Sandstone and Gazelles.
  - *Picture rugs:* Mansaf Feast, Rainbow Stairs of Amman, Ajloun Castle, Wadi Mujib, Dana Village and Knafeh.
- **Morocco** gets twelve more.
  - *Patterns and crafts:* Rabat Embroidery, Carved Plaster, Mint Tea, Goats in the Argan Trees, Brass Trays and Leather Poufs.
  - *Picture rugs:* Moroccan Doors, Essaouira Harbour, Jemaa el-Fna by Night, Riad Courtyard, Todra Gorge and Surf at Taghazout.
- Search finds the new rugs by name ("goats", "surf", "knafeh"), and they're in the tray menu under Pattern.

If you have 1.16.0 or later, this update is a few MB and restarting takes a few seconds.

**From 1.21:** the rug can move to a second screen (drag it across, or use "Move to the other screen" in the menu), the Invisible rug looks right on portrait and spanned screens, and Persia and Türkiye grew to 24 rugs each.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
