A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.24.0: tell us how you like it**
- Once, after you've used Desktop Carpet for a while, a small note appears in a corner of your screen: give it 1 to 5 stars and, if you like, a few words and a name to show with them.
- It asks only once on each computer, never over a full-screen game, film or presentation, and it doesn't take the keyboard from what you're doing. Skip it or close it and it won't ask again.
- Some comments may appear on desktopcarpet.com, with your name only if you add one. The note says so right under the comment box.
- It sends your stars, your words, the name if you typed one, the app version, Windows or Mac and the app's language: not your key, your email or anything else about you.

If you have 1.16.0 or later, this update is a few MB and restarting takes a few seconds.

**From 1.23:** anonymous usage statistics, with an off switch in the panel (**Send anonymous usage statistics**).

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
