A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.7.0: the Mexico pack**
*Desert to jungle, one rug at a time.* Twelve new Mexican rugs, in their own section of the panel:
- **Woven:** Sarape, Greca, Blue Tile, Agave Fields.
- **Folk:** Sugar Skull, Monarch Butterflies, Painted Jaguar.
- **Hooked wool,** with a pink and marigold sawtooth border: Greetings from Mexico, Pyramid, Colorful Street, Trajinera, Tulum Beach.

**From 1.6.0: the Canada pack**
*From sea to sea, one rug at a time.* Twelve new Canadian rugs, in their own section of the panel:
- **Woven and pieced:** Maple Leaf, Maple Leaf Quilt, Rocky Mountains, Atlantic Tartan.
- **Folk:** Moose Lake, Canada Geese, Loon at Dawn.
- **Hooked wool,** Canada's own rug craft, with a red and white sawtooth border: Greetings from Canada, Hockey Rink, Northern Lights, Lighthouse, Polar Bear.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
