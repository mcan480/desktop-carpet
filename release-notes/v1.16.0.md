A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.16.0: smaller, and much smaller updates**
- **Updates are now a few MB.** When a release only brings new rugs and fixes, Desktop Carpet downloads just those (about 5 MB) instead of the whole app (100–250 MB), and restarting takes a few seconds. If a new version ever fails to start, Desktop Carpet goes back to the one before by itself.
- **A smaller app.** The Windows installer is 100 MB instead of 123 MB and takes 303 MB instead of 347 MB on disk. The Mac download is 177 MB instead of 266 MB, and the app takes 477 MB instead of 561 MB. When a full update is needed, a Mac now downloads only the half made for its processor (about 125 MB instead of 242 MB).
- **A new update card in the panel:** how many MB have come down, and how long the restart will take.
- **An update screen:** after "Restart and update", a little rug is woven while the new version goes in, and it says so when it's done.
- The showroom's large rug pictures are now drawn by the app itself, sharper than before.

This update is the last full one: from now on, most updates are small.

**From 1.15.0:** the Mediterranean pack, 64 rugs from Italy, Greece, Spain and France.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
