A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.4.0**
- **Fringe.** Every rug now has wool tassels on its short ends, knotted in bundles like the warp threads of a real rug. They lie on the floor, and when you lift the edge they swing down with it. (The Invisible rug stays tassel-free, so it doesn't give itself away.)
- From 1.3.x: three Halloween rugs (Pumpkin Patch, Spider Web, Ghost Night).

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
