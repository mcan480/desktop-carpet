A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. Now on Windows and Mac.

**What's new in 1.1.0**
- **Desktop Carpet for Mac.** One app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 12 or newer). The rug lies on your desktop icons, under your app windows, on every Space. Its menu lives in the menu bar.
- The same license works on both: activate your key on a Mac and it counts as one of your computers.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. You only do this once; later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
