A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it. On Windows and Mac.

- **Japan:** Seigaiha Waves, Asanoha, Boro, Koi Pond, Gold Screen, Shibori, Zen Garden, Fuji and Sakura, Kyoto Lane, Greetings from Japan, The Great Wave, Maneki-neko, Torii Path and Sushi.
- **China:** Blue and White, Lucky Clouds, Shanghai Deco, Ningxia Peony, Ink Bamboo, Paper-Cut, Imperial Waves, Mahjong, Panda, The Great Wall, Greetings from China, Lantern Festival, Guilin and Dim Sum.
- **Its own room:** black lacquer with a vermilion frame and a tab for each country. The rug you're using hangs large with where it comes from, and pointing at another one previews it there. The first time you open the panel after updating, it opens on the new pack.
- Türkiye now has its own tab next to Persia, and every pack is in the tray menu under Pattern.

**From 1.13.0:** the Türkiye pack, 12 Anatolian carpets and picture rugs.

**Windows:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

**Mac:** download `DesktopCarpet-Mac.dmg`, open it and drag Desktop Carpet into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the app: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down and click *Open Anyway*. Later versions update themselves.

Then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com.
