A rug for your desktop. Grab it, lift a corner and sweep your files under it.

**What's new in 1.0.3**
- No more frozen rug: grabbing and dragging keep working even when Windows stops sending mouse events to the desktop (after overlays such as the NVIDIA one, menus or switching windows).

**Install:** download `DesktopCarpet-Setup.exe` below and run it, then paste the license key you got at https://desktopcarpet.com. If you already have Desktop Carpet, it updates itself: look for "Restart and update" in the panel.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose *More info* → *Run anyway*.
