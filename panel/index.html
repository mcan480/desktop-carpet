    How to use it
    • Grab the carpet anywhere and drag. The edge lifts, let go and it drops.
    • Drag files next to it, then pull the carpet over them.
    • Double-click flattens it. Scroll over it to turn it, Ctrl + scroll to resize.
    • Right-click the carpet or the tray icon for quick options.
